Booking terms

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

The Magnifying Society runs The O'Brien File, an outdoor scavenger hunt in Bondi, Sydney. These terms apply to every booking made on this website. If you have a question, write to us at hello@themagnifyingsociety.com.au.

Bookings and payment

  • Sessions run on Saturdays at 10:00 am and last about 90 minutes.
  • Tickets are sold per person. Prices are in Australian dollars and are shown before you pay.
  • You pay in full online when you book. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details.
  • Your booking is confirmed once payment goes through and you receive our confirmation email.

Changing your date

You can move your booking to another Saturday for free, up to 48 hours before your session. Reply to your confirmation email or write to us. Changes are subject to availability.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Tickets aren't refundable if you cancel, miss your session or arrive too late to start with the group.
  • If the weather is too wet to play, we cancel the session and refund you in full. We'll let you know by email as early as we can.
  • If we have to cancel a session for any other reason, you can choose a full refund or a new date.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with and can take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Who can play

  • The game is for players aged 13 and over.
  • Players under 15 must play with an adult (18+). Adults are responsible for the under-18s in their group.
  • Teams of 2 to 6 are formed on the day.

On the day

  • Please arrive at the meeting point 10 minutes early. The game starts at 10:00 am.
  • The route covers about 4 km on footpaths, streets and the beachfront, with stairs and some steep sections. It isn't suitable for wheelchairs.
  • The game is played in public places that stay open to traffic and other people. Follow road rules, use crossings, and take care of yourself and your group.
  • Bring a charged phone (one per team), comfortable shoes, water and sun protection.
  • We may ask a player to leave the game if their behaviour puts others at risk or spoils the game for other teams. No refund applies in that case.
  • We may adjust the route or the game on the day, for example because of works, events or safety.

Your responsibility

You take part at your own risk, to the extent the law allows. Please only play if you're fit to walk the route. You're responsible for your own belongings during the game.

Your consumer rights

Our services come with guarantees that can't be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms limits those rights.

Private groups

Private sessions for birthdays, parties and companies are arranged separately. We'll confirm the price and conditions in writing before you book.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version shown when you book applies to your booking. These terms are governed by the laws of New South Wales.
